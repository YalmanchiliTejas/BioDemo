How to read this report

Both case studies compare a traditional workflow with an AI operating layer over frozen, recorded benchmark artifacts. Every value below is read from those artifacts or produced by evaluating the existing deterministic agents over the frozen datasets. Nothing is estimated for presentation.

  • Missing measurements are shown as Not measured together with the recorded reason — never as zero or a placeholder.
  • Every result carries a classification: Verified, Observed, Modeled, Predicted or Not measured.
  • Replay timelines follow each case's recorded clock. They are not wall-clock traces and imply no synchronization between the two workflows.
  • Modeled campaign figures are projections under documented assumptions, not measured human performance.
Verified

Checked against an independent ground truth or the published source.

Observed

Directly measured in the dataset or recorded in execution logs.

Modeled

Produced by the documented simulation or workflow assumptions.

Predicted

Estimated by a model and not experimentally confirmed.

Not measured

No recorded benchmark output supports this value.
