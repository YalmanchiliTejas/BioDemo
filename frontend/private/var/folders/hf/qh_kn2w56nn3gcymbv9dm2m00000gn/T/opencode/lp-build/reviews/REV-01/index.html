Rationale

Hypothesis

Under the current open-loop feed, PM-204 delivered 14.2% less than setpoint after the T+264.4h pressure event and the control system did not compensate . The most-likely cause is tubing over-occlusion after WO-8819 . A gravimetric mass-balance cascade would correct delivered mass regardless of tubing slip, and a lower interlock would stop the pump before tubing damage escalates .

Expected result

Calculated

+5.1 yield-index pts (illustrative)

Simulated range +2.8 to +6.4 pts across 200 OPT-104 runs. Model not validated against plant outcomes; not a probability of success.

Constraints & owner

  • Requires load cell on feed tote (installed on BR-UP-01).
  • Applies from MES-26074 onward; not retroactive to MES-26073.
  • Implementation owner: Alex Rivera via DeltaV recipe change control.

Supporting evidence

What evidence would change this recommendation

  • Tubing teardown showing normal wear (would weaken the over-occlusion hypothesis behind the interlock change).
  • Gravimetric slip data from PM-204 at 184 RPM within ±3% (open-loop may then be adequate with CAPA-01 alone).
  • Loop-tuning trials showing the cascade oscillates at low feed rates.
