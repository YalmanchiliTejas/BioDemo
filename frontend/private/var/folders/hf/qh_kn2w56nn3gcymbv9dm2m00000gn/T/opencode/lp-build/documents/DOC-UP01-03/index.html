Investigation Summary: Feed Droop and Head-Pressure Exceedance, BR-UP-01

Deviation Q-DEV-031 · Batch MES-26073 · Prepared by Alex Rivera (MSAT) with Root Cause agent assistance

1. Purpose and scope

Human-written

This summary documents the investigation of deviation Q-DEV-031 on Skid BR-UP-01, batch MES-26073 (AB-mAb-014, campaign CMP-26-10). It covers the feed-flow droop and PM-204 head-pressure exceedance, the evidence reviewed, the most-likely cause and proposed CAPAs. It does not constitute a batch disposition decision.

2. Event description

Agent-draftedObservation

At T+264.4 h PM-204 discharge head pressure rose to 1.48 bar against a 1.20 bar ceiling, and delivered feed flow fell from 16.88 to 14.48 mL/min (−14.2%) within two minutes.

Agent-draftedObservation

The operator acknowledged the deviation at eBR step 05.4 with FEED_SP 16.90 mL/min and FEED_PV 14.48 mL/min.

Agent-drafted · human-editedUnknown

A 42-minute historian gap exists at T+182.0–182.7 h caused by a PI buffer overflow. No values from this window are used in any calculation in this report.

3. Investigation and evidence

Agent-draftedObservation

Work order WO-8819, performed 48 h before the event, records an increase of the PM-204 occlusion clamp torque from 0.42 to 0.55 N·m. Dynamic slip verification was not performed because the fixture was unavailable.

Human-writtenObservation

SOP-UP-014 Rev 7 §7.2 requires a dynamic slip verification at operating RPM after any occlusion adjustment, with gravimetric acceptance of ±3% of setpoint.

Agent-draftedObservation

The pump-head OEM manual states that increased occlusion shortens tubing service life (400–500 h nominal) and raises discharge pressure.

Agent-drafted · human-editedObservation

Harvest sample QC-948 returned an HPLC titer of 3.42 g/L, below the 3.80 g/L alert limit. System suitability passed. Second-person review is pending.

4. Most-likely root cause (pending QA)

Agent-drafted · human-editedHypothesis

The most-likely cause is over-occlusion of PM-204 tubing following WO-8819, without the required slip verification, leading to accelerated tubing fatigue, elevated discharge pressure and reduced delivered feed. This has not been confirmed by physical inspection of the removed tubing.

Agent-draftedContradicted

Alternative causes reviewed and not supported by evidence: feed media lot FM-2207 out of specification (all attributes within specification); DO probe drift (redundant probes agree within 0.6%).

5. Product impact

Human-writtenUnknown

Titer is below the alert limit; other in-process results are within specification except osmolality, which is near its upper limit. SEC monomer has not been tested, so aggregation impact is unknown. Lot MES-26073 remains on QA hold.

6. Proposed CAPA

Agent-draftedProposal

CAPA-01: exchange the PM-204 tubing spool and perform dynamic slip verification before return to service (owner R. Chen, due 2026-11-07). CAPA-02: amend SOP-UP-014 §7.2 so occlusion work orders cannot be closed without a slip-verification record (owner A. Rivera, due 2026-11-28). Both are proposals and have not been entered into QMS.
