Problem evidence: Open analysis AN-104
Hypothesis AHypothesis

Mechanical tubing fatigue / slip on PM-204

Supported · not established

Over-occlusion after the WO-8819 clamp adjustment, on tubing near the end of its rated life, raised discharge pressure and reduced delivered volume per revolution.

Supporting · 5

  • R² = 0.941 between feed loss and head pressure across 3 batches (n = 237 hourly points, imputed excluded). Correlation, not causal proof.

    Calculated

  • WO-8819 raised clamp torque 0.42 → 0.55 N·m ~48 h before the spike; dynamic slip check not performed.

    Observation

  • SOP-UP-014 §7.2 requires a slip verification after any occlusion adjustment.

    Observation

  • Tubing at 412 h of a 400–500 h OEM service life; OEM notes higher occlusion shortens life and raises pressure.

    Observation

  • PT-204 pressure transmitter calibrated Oct 3, as-found +0.3% FS — the pressure reading itself is credible.

    Observation

Contradicting · 1

  • MES-26072 declined 2.8 pts with no clamp adjustment and normal pressure — tubing slip does not explain that batch.

    Observation

What would change our mind

  • Gravimetric slip test on the removed tubing at 0.55 N·m shows delivery within ±3% of setpoint.
  • Fresh tubing at 0.42 N·m reproduces the same pressure rise at the same RPM.
  • Feed-loss-adjusted yield model leaves most of the 6.1-pt gap unexplained.
Hypothesis BContradicted

Raw feed media lot variation (FM-2207)

Contradicted

A nutrient-composition difference in the feed lot reduced productivity.

Contradicting · 3

  • CoA within specification: osmolality 312 mOsm/kg (290–330), glucose 198 g/L (190–210).

    Observation

  • Same lot FM-2207 fed MES-26072, which showed no T+264h feed drop.

    Observation

  • Titer trend diverges only after the feed-flow drop, not from feed start.

    Observation

What would change our mind

  • Trace-element or hydrolysate assay shows FM-2207 outside historical range (not on CoA today).
  • An alternate-lot batch reproduces the decline with normal feed delivery.
Hypothesis CContradicted

Dissolved-oxygen sensor drift

Contradicted

A drifting DO probe caused control to under-aerate, limiting cell productivity.

Contradicting · 2

  • Redundant probes DO_A and DO_B agree within 0.6% over T+200–280h.

    Observation

  • DO stays inside the 35–45% band on all three batches.

    Observation

What would change our mind

  • Off-line DO calibration check shows both probes biased in the same direction (common-mode drift).
