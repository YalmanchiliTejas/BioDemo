Practical constraints · Candidate A

  • Pump speed within OEM tubing limit (≤ 200 RPM)
  • Head pressure stays below the 1.20 bar ceiling
  • Feed-tote load cell LC-204 installed on BR-UP-01 skid
  • LC-204 calibration current
  • DeltaV cascade block available in the current control-system version
  • Change control raised for recipe and control strategy
  • Durham recipient loop can be mapped (TT-006 item 2.7)

4 met · 3 open · 1 unverified. Open items must close before implementation, not before review.

Assumptions

  1. Feed-loss-to-yield relationship from AN-104 (3 batches) holds for future batches; small sample, wide uncertainty.
  2. Hypothesis A (tubing slip) is the main driver of the feed shortfall. If tests TP-01/TP-02 contradict it, this estimate no longer applies.
  3. Controller holds delivered mass within ±2% of setpoint; tuning not yet tested on this skid.
  4. No change to media, cell line, temperature profile or harvest timing.
  5. Range reflects bootstrap resampling of the 3-batch fit (5th–95th percentile); it is not a probability that the outcome will fall inside it.

Outcome measure after implementation

Primary: |feed PV − SP| ≤ 3% throughout Phase 05 on the next 3 batches.

Secondary: PM-204 head pressure ≤ 1.20 bar; normalized yield index compared against the +3.2 to +6.8 pt estimate.

Review point: after MES-26076 harvest. If yield gain is below +3.2 pts, revisit hypothesis A.

Rationale
Feed shortfall tracks head pressure (R² = 0.941, correlation) and the tubing-slip mechanism is plausible. A mass-balance loop corrects delivered feed regardless of tubing condition.
Expected result
+5.1 pts normalized yield (illustrative range +3.2 to +6.8) and feed delivery within ±3% of setpoint.
Constraints
Change control, LC-204 calibration check, operator training and Durham loop mapping (TT-006) remain open.
