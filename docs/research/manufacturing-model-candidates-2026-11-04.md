# Manufacturing model candidates beyond freeze drying

Research date: 2026-11-04. Primary documentation/source screen; no installation, runtime integration, or predictive validation performed. Read `Main_Culture_andETHOS.md`, the root glossary, domain guidance and ADR 0001. These are recommendations for further qualification, not already available BioDemo capabilities.

## Recommendation

Build breadth around three different equipment/data contexts: **heat exchangers and utilities (IDAES), crystallization and solid isolation (PharmaPy), and chromatographic purification (CADET)**. These cover process manufacturing across chemicals, pharmaceutical drug substance and biologics. They do not establish coverage of discrete manufacturing such as machining, assembly or injection molding. A machinery label alone is insufficient to select a predictive model; material properties, operating regime, parameter evidence, measured outputs and intended question also matter.

The parent code audit reports that the host catalog has primary-drying and fermentation branches, while the service creates a default freeze-drying `ModelCatalog()`. Existing PenSim/SMPL examples are restricted simulator demonstrations. This screen does not turn those examples or the candidates below into a general qualified model-selection service.

## 1. Heat exchanger performance: IDAES — first next case

**Verified capability.** The official [0D heat-exchanger documentation](https://idaes-pse.readthedocs.io/en/stable/reference_guides/model_libraries/generic/unit_models/heat_exchanger.html) defines hot/cold control volumes, energy/material balances, `Q = UA ΔT` and a default logarithmic mean temperature difference. Beyond inlet states, two specifications normally close the model. Importantly, `U` has no constitutive constraint by default: selecting this unit does not supply a fouling or geometry-based heat-transfer law. The [official worked example](https://idaes.github.io/examples-pse/latest/Examples/UnitModels/Operations/heat_exchanger_0D.html) demonstrates steam heating a benzene/toluene stream with separate property packages and an IPOPT solve. This is a computational example, not measured plant validation.

**Proposed case.** Compare clean and degraded heat-transfer performance, or source/receiving equipment duty limits, using measured flow, inlet/outlet temperatures, composition/phase, pressure and known area. Estimate effective `UA` on calibration runs; evaluate held-out outlet temperatures and duty on different operating points. Treat low effective `UA` as a diagnostic observation; it does not uniquely identify fouling rather than bypass, sensor error or changed properties. Start with a steady-state model; use a separate transient formulation and holdup/wall data for startup questions.

**Setup/license.** [Official installation instructions](https://idaes-pse.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorials/getting_started/mac_osx.html) separate Python installation from solver/property extensions (`idaes get-extensions`); OS/architecture and optional solver availability require a smoke test. The [source license](https://github.com/IDAES/idaes-pse/blob/main/LICENSE.md) permits source/binary redistribution under notice and nonendorsement conditions and includes an enhancements clause; preserve its exact text and dependency notices. A framework installation does not provide product-specific thermodynamic or reaction parameters.

**Why first.** Our assessment: the narrow balance calculation is inspectable, useful beyond one product family, and can begin with common historian/equipment records. The main friction is trustworthy synchronized measurements and properties, rather than inventing kinetics.

## 2. Crystallization → filtration/drying: PharmaPy

**Verified capability.** [PharmaPy's source repository](https://github.com/CryPTSys/PharmaPy) supports dynamic drug-substance operations and batch/continuous/hybrid flowsheets. The [official guide](https://pharmapy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/examples/PharmaPy_guide.html) specifies chemical-property JSON, reaction kinetics, crystallization nucleation/growth/dissolution and solubility parameters; drying needs additional heat-capacity, vapor-pressure and vaporization properties. This is **PharmaPy**, not the separate pharmacometrics project **Pharmpy**. The original [2024 integrated-purification study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11225065/) develops crystallization, filtration, deliquoring, washing and drying models and simulates an integrated two-stage crystallization/carousel process. Its digital design result is not proof of a prevented commercial failure.

**Proposed case.** Assess how cooling or seed changes alter crystal-size distribution and downstream isolation duration/recovery. Minimum credible inputs include API/solvent identity, solubility versus temperature, nucleation/growth evidence, initial seed size distribution, concentrations and temperature histories. Adding filtration needs slurry/cake characteristics, filter area, pressure and resistance evidence; drying adds solvent/moisture, gas/thermal conditions and transfer parameters. Begin with one crystallizer; qualify downstream units separately before coupling them. Generic machine type and temperature alone cannot predict product-specific crystal kinetics, polymorph formation or cake behavior.

**Setup/license.** The [source installation instructions](https://github.com/CryPTSys/PharmaPy/blob/master/install_instructions.txt) use a conda environment, requirements file and source installation, and show a Python 3.9 manual example; that example is not evidence of compatibility with this repository's runtime. The [source license](https://github.com/CryPTSys/PharmaPy/blob/master/LICENSE.md) permits source/binary redistribution subject to notices/nonendorsement and an enhancements clause. Pin the actual source revision and dependencies before evaluation. The PharmaPy-org fork also exists; do not silently mix fork code, historical docs and study-specific extensions.

## 3. Column purification: CADET

**Verified capability.** The [general-rate-model documentation](https://cadet.github.io/master/modelling/unit_operations/general_rate_model.html) includes column transport, particle transport and binding, assuming homogeneous column cross sections and neglecting radial bulk transport. Consequently, an unexplained peak shift is not automatically a modeled channeling defect. The [Core README](https://github.com/cadet/CADET-Core/blob/master/README.rst) documents conda installation. A [March 2026 maintainer announcement](https://forum.cadet-web.de/t/cadet-core-is-now-on-pypi-we-created-a-metapackage/1404) also documents wheels and a `cadet` metapackage combining compatible Core, Process and Python packages. Verify wheel/platform coverage for the selected release.

**Proposed case.** Predict changed breakthrough/elution under flow, column-size, load or gradient changes, and compare proposed collection windows. Required evidence includes column dimensions, bed/particle porosity and size, component/feed concentrations, flow/gradient/inlet schedules, detector/assay traces, and suitable binding/isotherm and transport parameters. Use calibration runs to identify parameters and reserve genuinely different conditions for evaluation. Do not claim protein-specific purity or recovery from a generic binding model without component assays. A low-parameter lumped model may be preferable when data cannot identify a general rate model.

**License version trap.** The current [v5.1.2 release listing](https://github.com/cadet/CADET-Core/releases) distinguishes stable and v6 prereleases. Direct inspection found **GNU GPL v3** in [v5.1.2 LICENSE.txt](https://github.com/cadet/CADET-Core/blob/v5.1.2/LICENSE.txt), whereas [master LICENSE.txt](https://github.com/cadet/CADET-Core/blob/master/LICENSE.txt) contains **GNU Affero GPL v3**. Do not repeat a generic GPL label across releases. Record the chosen release and exact dependency licenses before deciding how to package or host it; this screen does not determine legal obligations for a proposed integration.

## Other credible breadth, with limits

**DWSIM** is worth a later flowsheet/property-package evaluation rather than another bespoke solver. Official [API documentation](https://dwsim.org/help/api/) catalogs operations and distinguishes subscription extras; [CAPE-OPEN documentation](https://dwsim.org/help/main/60-cape-open-subsystem.html) describes interoperability with external property packages and unit operations. The inspected [source-license policy](https://github.com/DanWBR/dwsim/blob/windows/CONTRIBUTING.md) states GPLv3, and the [download page](https://dwsim.org/download/) distinguishes open-source core and editions with extras. Do not assume every advertised operation is in the same license tier or that COM-based interoperability is portable across OSes. No DWSIM execution was tested here.

**FMI/FMU interoperability** can reuse an organization's existing approved equipment model. [OpenModelica's official FMI guide](https://openmodelica.org/doc/OpenModelicaUsersGuide/latest/fmitlm.html) documents model exchange/co-simulation and FMU export; the inspected development guide lists FMI 2 support and experimental FMI 3 support. [FMPy's source README](https://github.com/CATIA-Systems/FMPy) documents simulation of FMI 1/2/3 FMUs from Python across Windows/Linux/macOS. This is a transport/runtime option, not an available manufacturing model: the actual FMU's physics, platform binary, license, exposed variables and validation remain prerequisites.

## Selection and evaluation contract proposed for later implementation

Simple alternative: a capability table plus deterministic eligibility checks presents candidate calculations and missing inputs. Ambitious alternative: an agent resolves equipment/data context, chooses among eligible model families, calibrates and executes them in isolated runtimes. Prefer the simple gate first; an agent can help map messy records and explain alternatives, while numerical eligibility, units and permissions remain explicit checks.

For either alternative, record equipment/regime applicability, supported question, required signals and units, required parameters and provenance, calibration bounds, known exclusions, solver/license/version, and measured evaluation status. Return “insufficient evidence” when the available records cannot distinguish candidate mechanisms or identify parameters. Preserve raw records in their existing systems; preserve calculation inputs, model revision, transformations, solver status and uncertainty separately from interpretations and recommendations. A model recommendation does not authorize changing equipment or recipes.

Assess all four pillars jointly: discover authorized records to reduce preparation; expose applicability, assumptions and held-out error for trust; propose a specific verification run or reviewed adjustment for action; reuse existing equipment records and modular runtimes for adoption. Measure actual setup/manual-input effort, evidence review time, prediction error under changed conditions, correct abstention and integration burden. No accuracy, time-saving, compliance or avoided-loss claim has been validated by this research.
