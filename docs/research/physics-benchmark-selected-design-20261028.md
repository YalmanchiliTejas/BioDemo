# Selected benchmark design with model updates deferred

28 October 2026. Experiment selection and development sensitivity evidence; **no new AI A/B run or confirmatory result**. This design follows the user's instruction to keep the present models and defer model updates.

## Decision

Select three linked experiment families: transient prediction, constrained operating decisions, and receiving-recipe adaptation. Keep the main comparison **the same AI without a supplied physics model versus the same AI with the frozen physics tool**. Choose the task distribution from the mechanisms and intended decisions before inspecting agent performance. A benchmark can decisively test a scoped hypothesis; selecting experiments cannot guarantee that the hypothesis is true.

The strongest claim available from the present simulation tools is usefulness within specified simulated process regimes. Real manufacturing accuracy and real site transfer need independent empirical evidence. Shared-equation evaluator/tool agreement, even with new seeds, cannot supply that evidence.

## The three selected experiments

| Aspect | Selected experiment | Main outcome | Why this is more informative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Predictions | Forecast the time course after a known change in operating inputs. PenSim: declared feed/aeration schedules within its admitted96h partial-process envelope. SMPL: balanced-flow changes in its24h upstream subsystem. Include short, medium and end-of-horizon forecasts; both agents see identical history and planned commands. | Error across every predeclared variable and horizon, including oxygen/cells as well as product. Normalize using a scale fixed from permitted development/source data, never a selectively chosen favorable test endpoint. | Tests dynamic responses rather than extrapolation around an almost unchanged trajectory. The expected advantage comes from the modeled balances; whether it occurs is unknown. |
| Operating decisions | Choose a complete bounded feed/air plan in PenSim or balanced-throughput/resource plan in SMPL, under predeclared command/resource/state limits. Use materially different admissible options rather than only±1%. | Constraint satisfaction first; then product objective and regret relative to the private finite action grid. Report the actual objective improvement versus KEEP separately. | Gives decisions room to matter and exposes trade-offs; avoids counting a slightly different forecast as an operating improvement. A development audit must show that the task is not just “always pick the maximum input.” |
| Recipe transfer | Give completed source history and known receiving actuator limits/schedules. Withhold receiving observations. Ask the agents to retain or adapt the source plan, commit once, and evaluate receiving consequences. Keep modeled biology and geometry fixed. | Receiving forecast error, constraint satisfaction and committed-plan objective relative to copying the source recipe. | Tests whether the agent can use process dynamics when the source plan encounters different operating capabilities. This is a **simulated transfer proxy**, not demonstrated vessel scale-up or site readiness. |

The existing four-action interface does not yet implement all these plans. More operating-input schedules/actions and trajectory submissions require benchmark adapter/tool/scorer work. That work changes the experiment interface, not equations, kinetic coefficients or the scientific model. It must be separately verified before execution. No such interface change was made in this selection work.

## Development checks performed before choosing test cases

An [exact input plan](../../backend/results/external-simulators/selection-20261028/development-plan.json), [all-point receipt](../../backend/results/external-simulators/selection-20261028/development-audit.json), and [reproduction script](../../backend/results/external-simulators/selection-20261028/development_audit.py) are retained. These use inspected development seeds0 and17 and perform **zero agent runs**. They are not held-out evaluation.

- PenSim: every pair of feed and air factors0.85/1.00/1.15, applied after60h and scored at96h, for both development seeds. All18 integrations completed, but **8 of18 triggered upstream oxygen flooring**. Such trajectories cannot be counted as clean oxygen-constrained decision evidence. In particular, apparent product improvements of roughly4.6–6.5% from reducing both feed and air include floor events. They are retained as model-limit findings, not promoted into benchmark wins. Floor-free feed-reduction options showed only about1.8–3.3% product change versus KEEP in these development cases. This is possible control headroom, not an AI benefit.
- SMPL: every pair of balanced-flow factor0.8/0.9/1.0 and inlet nutrient factor0.8/0.9/1.0, using pinned nominal initial conditions and a24h horizon. All9 integrations completed. Product outflow increased monotonically toward the largest tested inputs in this grid. That simple task does **not** yet demonstrate a meaningful competing choice; it can be retained as an easy control, but a harder constrained task must pass a development-only headroom check.
- An additional [constrained recovery audit](../../backend/results/external-simulators/selection-20261028/smpl-constrained-development-audit.json) retained all54 combinations of three initial cell levels, two inhibitor levels, three flow levels and three nutrient levels. Its source-controller cell lower bound and declared nutrient budget were fixed before running. Five of six initial-condition groups again chose the same best feasible action; the sixth had no feasible grid action. This is useful evidence for an explicit infeasibility/abstention task, but it does not yet establish diverse competitive operating choices. Do not remove the infeasible group to improve scores. All81 development integrations across both audits completed; no AI comparisons were run.
- The pinned SMPL glucose settings are near saturation relative to their Monod constant, so these input changes are not evidence of a glucose-limited regime. Do not invent starvation by silently rescaling units or coefficients. Detailed source evidence is in the [selection research note](physics-benchmark-experiment-selection-20261028.md).
- Hashes of the local physical model/adapter files and pinned upstream equations/controller files matched before and after the development check. No model update, unit correction, calibration change or parameter tuning was performed.

These findings constrain the design: prioritize admitted feed-response and flow-transient forecasts; make larger decisions conditional on meaningful feasible trade-offs; defer oxygen-floor-dependent stress claims. Do not label the current task suite definitive across all three aspects before those gates pass.

## Conditions for a credible final test

1. Freeze the current model versions, corrections and calibration rules. Preserve all existing positive and negative results. Defer new physics, parameter tuning, longer PenSim batches and model replacement. Any future source-only calibration already authorized by the frozen method must be fixed before agent inference and documented; receiving futures cannot determine it.
2. Use a separate development set for interface correctness, numerical admissibility and task headroom. It may establish that actions differ in consequence; it must not be used to select only scenarios where B wins. Record every examined setting and failed family.
3. Before generating the hidden evaluation set, lock the full scenario distribution, action/resource bounds, observation cutoffs, variables/horizons, scores, minimum useful effects, failure policy and sample-size/power plan. The number of independent process cases must be sized for those effects; extra AI repetitions do not replace independent cases. No optional stopping or extending sample size until a favorable result appears.
4. Include ordinary near-steady cases as a declared comparison stratum alongside transient/constraint cases. Publish each stratum and the overall result. If the claim concerns challenging regimes only, say so explicitly.
5. Give both arms identical raw information, the same AI/settings, memory-free sessions, the same nonphysics tools and the same inference/tool-call limits. B alone can run the frozen physics tool. An action must have the same meaning and apply the same operating limits in its tool and evaluator; no hidden true parameters or outcome ranking may enter the tool.
6. Numerical/domain checks apply independently of which arm wins. An agent selecting a documented invalid/infeasible action counts as a failed decision. A simulator failure that prevents scoring is a coverage failure and can block confirmation; do not remove it from the denominator or silently resample it away. Keep domain-limited stress families visible.
7. Estimate paired effects by independent process-case clusters, keeping repeated AI sessions and correlated tasks together. Record completions and runtime/tool costs. Report every predeclared target; no hiding oxygen degradation inside a favorable product average. If using several endpoints to make one headline claim, predeclare the joint decision rule and multiplicity treatment.
8. A claim of improvement in **all three aspects** requires each aspect to beat its locked useful-effect threshold with the specified uncertainty criterion, while meeting the constraint/completion/domain requirements. A tie or failed aspect yields a qualified/mixed result. If all three improve in simulation, the supported claim remains about those simulations.

The numeric decision limits, minimum useful effects and power-derived test-case count remain **to be locked after development qualification**, before hidden test generation. This document records the selected families and admission findings, not a completed preregistration or an already powered benchmark.

## Real measurements and deferred claims

Keep the existing condition-held-out CHO results visible: physics did not improve average measured VCD/glucose forecasts. Those inspected cultures cannot become a fresh positive benchmark by changing the subset, horizon or primary endpoint after seeing their errors. A new independent applicable measurement dataset can test the frozen model, but no such dataset has been selected or evaluated here.

Defer full230h PenSim optimization, new vessel geometry/mixing physics, different cell lines, downstream quality and real site-transfer readiness. None can be established by changing a label or an operating-input schedule. ## Product-direction trade-offs

The job is a readable, falsifiable answer for a beginner evaluating whether physics helps the same AI. Reusing models and benchmark infrastructure reduces setup and migration. Input/model hashes, failed-point receipts and held-out submission boundaries make verification possible. Decision consequences are scored separately from forecasts so the findings can guide a next step. No equipment writes or manufacturing approvals occur. The trade-off is that fixing models later restricts the challenges that can be admitted now; a narrower honest claim is preferable to treating solver artifacts or favorable selection as success. Human effort reduction and manufacturing savings remain unmeasured.
