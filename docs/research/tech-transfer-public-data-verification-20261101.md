# Public data verification for paired-scale technology transfer

Checked 1 November 2026. Primary records and actual small source files were inspected; no model calls, credentials, author contact or large archive downloads were used. Existing research notes and source manifests were reviewed first. This is dataset qualification research, not evidence that an agent predicts transfer outcomes or saves engineering time.

**Finding:** Sulfolobus is the strongest existing equipment/oxygen-transfer case, but supplies published summaries rather than raw process trajectories. Pichia supplies paired biological endpoints with inadequate oxygen-transfer characterization. A newly verified Trichoderma workbook provides genuine paired-scale gas measurements, but only one culture per scale and no raw product trajectory. None alone establishes a broad, replicated pharmaceutical site-transfer benchmark.

## Actual paired-scale gas workbook: Trichoderma reesei

[Zenodo record 5846028](https://zenodo.org/records/5846028) provides three workbooks; the [Paasikallio2017_CO2_data.xlsx download](https://zenodo.org/records/5846028/files/Paasikallio2017_CO2_data.xlsx?download=1) was retrieved and opened with openpyxl, inspecting both values and formulas. It is 731,057 bytes; MD5 `f6c552f3fba234edd224c6f9e1417ecc` matches the published checksum. SHA-256 is `af7742d58df66fb46e1ca2e6af32a5adf793714e92e703af9aec3b78a63ed81a`. [API metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/5846028) identifies CC BY 4.0. The unchanged workbook and attribution/hash manifest are preserved in [tech-transfer-data/paasikallio2017/](tech-transfer-data/paasikallio2017/source-manifest.json).

The record title refers to marine Trichoderma T2, but **this particular workbook explicitly references the earlier Paasikallio 2017 T. reesei study**. Do not merge its identity with the newer marine strain or treat the three record workbooks as matched scale replicates.

Actual workbook inspection:

Columns A–F are `Time (h)`, `Nitrogen`, `Oxygen`, `Argon`, `Carbon Dioxide`, `Ethanol`. Both sheets have strictly increasing numeric time rows and complete numeric values for those six columns. Worksheet dimensions (4,791/4,121 rows ×20 columns) include empty formatted space and must not be counted as measurements. Columns H–M contain an `Air input` heading and two numeric rows of inlet-gas reference values; the second row has no explicit statistical label. These are not an inlet-flow trajectory. Gas-channel headings do not state concentration units; their numerical ranges resemble percent composition, which remains an interpretation requiring confirmation. There are no raw product, biomass, feed, liquid-volume, pH, DO, rpm, torque, kLa or power time series in this file.

The [associated original paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5504852/) identifies T. reesei VTT D-161646 at 1/10/250 L; both 1/250 L use Meridianstar pectin, while 10 L uses Sigma. Culture conditions include 35°C, pH4, approximately0.5vvm; 1L agitation350–500rpm and pilot125–200rpm. The pilot reactor has350L maximum working volume;250L is the culture scale. Equipment includes Biostat Q and New Brunswick IF400. Feed initiation differed between1/250L; this is not a controlled scale-only intervention. Table3 reports endpoint product18/21/15g/L and process durations215/185/168h, whereas abstract/narrative give other pilot product/recovery values. Preserve these conflicts rather than selecting one silently. The workbook extends beyond the tabulated168h pilot duration. Paper biomass error bars are analytical replicates, not evidence of multiple independent pilot batches. Geometry, measured kLa, power, pressure and detailed feed histories are insufficiently specified for an independently identified mechanistic scale-up model.

**Feasible use (inference):** freeze the 1L donor data and permitted receiver context, then assess limited receiver gas-profile predictions or batch-phase interpretation against the250L measurements. A single paired run cannot support strong statistical generalization. Later receiving gas records must be hidden for a forecasting task; early receiving measurements change the task to adaptation. Product prediction would require separately obtained raw HPLC/biomass/feed data, calibrated kinetics, and reconciliation of paper endpoints. Do not represent gas composition as product titer or every recorded sample as an independent batch.

## Existing candidates rechecked

**Sulfolobus, actual2→20→200L working volumes.** The [primary paper](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioengineering-and-biotechnology/articles/10.3389/fbioe.2023.1160012/full) reports measured water kLa at75°C and published biological summaries. Table2 includes receiving kLa40.5/41.1h⁻¹ and selected receiver settings; gassed power is calculated, not measured. The [official supplement API](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/23897628) currently lists one563,784-byte DOCX, public/download-enabled, CC BY4.0. Local DOCX/table extraction confirms equipment dimensions and figure images, not raw transition/fermentation tables. Rushton impeller diameters52/95/200mm and counts2/3/4 permit equipment-aware checks. Donor ring-sparger calibration has only two agitation points at one gas flow: gas-flow and geometry effects are not identifiable. Water-to-broth applicability is unvalidated. At200L viability is absent and off-gas measurements failed. The data statement offers raw data through authors; it is not a public raw archive. Best use: small source-grounded parameter/assumption audit with held-out receiver summaries. See [existing detailed curation](fermentation/sulfolobus-sources.md).

**Pichia, nominal10→100→1,000L.** [Liu2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4726300/) supplies Table2 paired enzyme/biomass endpoints and figure curves; local archived XML contains no supplementary attachment or raw-data deposit. Initial medium volumes are3.5/35/400L with inoculum wording requiring care; nominal vessel size is not culture volume. Published vessel, rpm/airflow envelopes and pressure/feed conditions lack impeller geometry, measured kLa/OUR, power and off-gas. The30L oxygen-versus-pressure comparison is a separate experiment. The1,000L112h table entry is explicitly not a measured peak/harvest. It is experimental paper-summary evidence under CC BY4.0, suitable for extraction, missing-input detection and qualified empirical endpoint comparisons, not mechanistic productivity validation. See [existing detailed curation](fermentation/pichia-sources.md).

**Stuttgart NISTCHO mixing study.** The [live DaRUS API](https://darus.uni-stuttgart.de/api/datasets/:persistentId/?persistentId=doi:10.18419/DARUS-6466) now lists11 public unrestricted files, including `All Data.xlsx`(41,868bytes), nine figure CSVs and `Sub.Table1.tab`; license CC BY4.0. Thus the prior shortlist's description of only figure tables was incomplete. Directly fetched [Figure2.csv](https://darus.uni-stuttgart.de/api/access/datafile/659817) contains three mixing-time summaries: reference11.6±0.5s, SMCB150.2±5s, pDs128.8±6s. All cultivation conditions are3L systems. These are altered-geometry scale-down experiments, not measured donor/production-scale pairs. The workbook was not opened in this review; raw replicated trajectory coverage cannot be inferred from its filename.

**OWL CHO.** [Zenodo20829178](https://zenodo.org/records/20829178) describes24 experimental cultures at0.55–0.8L working volume,38 inline and10 offline variables. Existing [local manifest](../../backend/benchmarks/cho/source_manifest.json) preserves archive/workbook/CSV hashes and CC BY4.0; the [existing file inspection](../../backend/benchmarks/cho/dataset_profile.md) records process/gas/feed/cell/metabolite schema. No large redownload was needed. Paired R01/R02 cultures are parallel small reactors, not verified donor/receiving-scale transfers. Useful forecasting evidence, insufficient paired industrial transfer evidence.

**IndPenSim and other executable models.** The [original dataset record](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/pdnjz7zz5x/2) explicitly identifies simulated100,000L penicillin trajectories. It is synthetic single-scale evidence even though model development used industrial observations. No2.5GB download or schema inspection was performed here. Existing [physical-model source review](physical-model-evaluation-datasets.md) covers the Python port, SMPL mAb and CC-Sim; those are simulators requiring qualification, not public measured transfer pairs. Telstar logs are measured freeze-drying cycles, not a paired upstream-bioprocess transfer dataset.

## Evaluation decision

Use Sulfolobus for a narrow equipment/oxygen-transfer reasoning audit and the verified Trichoderma workbook for a separately declared paired gas-profile task. Keep Pichia endpoints as an additional small empirical case with explicit missing inputs. A broader claim needs multiple independent donor/receiver runs, raw process and quality outcomes, identifiable model inputs and an independently frozen split. Public sources may already be in training data; isolate full papers, receiver settings/outcomes and this note from evaluated agents. Score measured targets only, and label unexecuted operating recommendations as proposals. This review did not rerun or assess existing agent studies and does not imply they are absent.
