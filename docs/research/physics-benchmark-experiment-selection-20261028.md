# Experiment selection with frozen physics models

28 October 2026. Research and protocol recommendations only: no model, code or dataset changes and no new AI runs were performed for this note. Select experiments because their mechanisms match existing model capabilities, not because inspected A/B results suggest a win. Improvement is a hypothesis to test, not a qualification requirement that may be achieved by reselecting cases.

## Question and fixed treatment

Compare **the same AI without a supplied physics model** against **the same AI plus the existing frozen physics-model tool**. Both receive identical raw process observations, known operating settings, permitted future plans, units and actions. Keep models, fitted coefficients, calibration rules, solver corrections, tool versions, inference settings and budgets frozen. A retains general numerical analysis and its learned knowledge; it receives no supplied equations, parameters, model predictions or simulator imports. B's access to the existing tool is the treatment. A deterministic model-only result can help interpret the experiment but must remain secondary to A/B.

The job is to establish where model access changes forecast errors or decisions under stated limits. Reuse existing sources/tools to limit setup; retain source hashes, cutoffs and failures for verification; measure decision consequences with explicit endpoints; and keep the experiment isolated from production systems. The trade-off is inspectable, bounded evidence rather than a plant-validation claim.

## What the frozen models actually cover

- **IndPenSim through the pinned PenSimPy port:** `e08fc71fef193448f3ee9a304187dc92b88f3acc`. Its equations couple substrate, biomass/production, oxygen, viscosity, gas composition and environmental effects. Exposed commands include sugar/oil feed, aeration, pressure, water, PAA and discharge. Original publication evidence concerns historical penicillin processes, not this port's arbitrary interventions. Inspect the [pinned equations](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/e08fc71fef193448f3ee9a304187dc92b88f3acc/pensimpy/ode/indpensim_ode_py.py), [controller/step implementation](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/e08fc71fef193448f3ee9a304187dc92b88f3acc/pensimpy/peni_env_setup.py), and [original study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25449107/). Retain the already disclosed native exponent/control-clock corrections and the **96-hour partial-process** boundary. Do not quietly restore a 230-hour endpoint whose numerical admission failed.
- **SMPL upstream:** `c816917eebfde59d525e325b56d16db07e499531`, original 17-state `UpModelHelper`. This models a continuous reactor/separator with cells, metabolites, mAb, volumes and temperature. Commands are four linked flows, inlet glucose/glutamine concentrations and coolant temperature. It does not include oxygen transfer or mixing gradients. See [pinned helper](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl/blob/c816917eebfde59d525e325b56d16db07e499531/smpl/envs/helpers/mab_helpers.py) and [original paper, Appendix A.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.08851#A1.SS3). Retain the **24-hour upstream** boundary; do not imply execution of its full downstream environment.
- **Existing fitted CHO batch model:** keep the already frozen fold-specific growth/glucose model and its parameter receipts unchanged. Its [implementation](../../backend/benchmarks/external_simulators/cho_real.py) is a lumped batch model, not SMPL's continuous process. Its real-data task is observational VCD/glucose prediction; it has no antibody, arbitrary intervention or site-transfer outcome target.

## Candidate experiment families

The following are **mechanism-based proposals**, not conclusions from observed agent performance.

| Family | Suitable experiments using frozen physics | Why model access might help | What must be reported |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prediction | Forecast several fixed future times within the admitted horizon, including planned command transitions. PenSim: oxygen and product/substrate response to declared feed/aeration/pressure schedules. SMPL: cells, glucose, mAb and external product flow after declared balanced-flow, inlet-composition or coolant changes. | Dynamic storage, nonlinear growth/inhibition, dilution and residence time can make straight-line continuation unreliable. | Errors for each target/horizon; failures and side effects; all predeclared regimes, including cases where a simple trend works well. |
| Simulated operating outcomes | Choose a bounded combination of controls under predeclared resource and state constraints. PenSim: sugar feed versus aeration/pressure. SMPL: balanced throughput versus inlet nutrient concentration/coolant. Commit before evaluator replay. | A candidate can improve product while worsening oxygen, nutrient depletion, temperature or resource use. Coupled balances may help compare these trade-offs. | Physical feasibility under the study's declared bounds, objective change versus KEEP, finite-grid regret, resource consumption and unsuccessful commitments. |
| Simulated recipe transfer | Provide completed source history and known receiving operating capabilities/plans, with no receiving observations. Change available aeration/pressure/feed delivery or balanced pump throughput at the same modeled geometry. Ask whether to retain or adapt the recipe. | The same recipe under changed operating limits can produce different dynamic responses even with unchanged biology and geometry. | Receiving-plan forecast error and simulated consequences of committed changes. Call it operating-condition recipe adaptation, with explicit uncertainty. |

**Ready at the current adapter boundary:** existing KEEP/feed-down/feed-up/air-up and balanced-flow-down/up/cool actions, existing within-horizon forecasts, existing declared receiving factors, and existing real-CHO prediction cases. New combinations of the upstream model's existing inputs or several scheduled intervention times can leave equations and coefficients unchanged, but still require new adapter/tool/protocol work, numerical admission and a fresh freeze. They must not be described as already supported by the present four-action interface.

For SMPL constant-volume cases, preserve `F_in + F_r = F_1` and `F_1 = F_r + F_2`; varying one flow independently can drain/fill a vessel and confound the intended comparison. Measure upstream harvested mass as the integral of separator mAb concentration times external outlet flow, in consistent units. Do not use the stock mixed-scaling productivity reward. For PenSim, distinguish terminal concentration from recovered mass; its discharge metadata/equations have a documented unit conflict. Resource budgets may be defined directly from commanded gas/feed quantities, but commercial energy, cost or quality claims require additional models/data.

Constraints must be declared independently of A/B outcomes and identified as experimental limits unless externally qualified. Avoid an automatically winning “always increase everything” task by using meaningful resource limits and coupled objectives; do not tune limits until B wins. Select a balanced predetermined set of regimes, controls and horizons rather than inspecting the winning action and constructing questions around it.

### Qualification of nutrient challenges in the pinned SMPL envelope

The existence of independent nutrient-input controls does **not** establish a nutrient-limited challenge within the pinned operating envelope. Inspection of the pinned configuration finds reactor glucose approximately **2467.7398** and inlet glucose approximately **1999.9987**, both declared mM by the upstream code, while `K_glc` is **0.75 mM**. Reactor glutamine is approximately **5.7679 mM**, versus `K_gln = 0.075 mM`. These values place the corresponding Monod terms near saturation. The source controller's roughly 0.8–1.0 nominal input range therefore does not justify claiming a glucose-limited regime. These are source-code/configuration observations, not independently validated physical concentrations or operating limits. Sources: [pinned helper equations/controller bounds](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl/blob/c816917eebfde59d525e325b56d16db07e499531/smpl/envs/helpers/mab_helpers.py) and [pinned configuration](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl/tree/c816917eebfde59d525e325b56d16db07e499531/smpl/configdata/mabenv).

Before selecting an SMPL nutrient-limited experiment, perform a development-only audit of parameter units, physical interpretation, admissible control headroom and response sensitivity. Do not change units/coefficients to manufacture limitation, stretch the envelope without evidence, or select cases according to B's observed advantage. If the existing frozen model and admissible commands remain saturated, retain that finding and use supported throughput/dilution or transient prediction questions instead; defer a stronger nutrient-limited task. PenSim feed/aeration challenges likewise remain conditional on a numerically admitted, **DO-floor-free** domain rather than apparent performance created by clipping.

## Real CHO measurements remain a separate empirical check

The [primary Zenodo dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/20829178) contains 24 CHO-K1 cultivations in paired reactors: nine batch experiments and three fed-batch experiments, with raw workbooks and aligned tables. The existing batch forecast uses raw VCD and glucose measurements; units/provenance and exact sample times must be retained. Keep both reactors of an EXP in the same held-out group. Existing frozen parameters may be conditioned on the permitted prefix by the already specified rule, without future-target fitting.

This real-data result **stays included even if the model-assisted agent performs worse**. Its previously inspected cultures and forecasts cannot be turned into a fresh confirmatory win by choosing a favorable subset, cutoff or target. Repeated AI sessions measure agent variability on the same experimental records; they do not create new biological replicates. Missing-target exclusions remain governed by the original structural rule. An independently collected future dataset could supply stronger evidence without changing the model, provided the experimental regime is applicable and its evaluation is frozen before inspecting outcomes.

The six fed-batch cultures are not a clean extension of the frozen batch task: future logged feed/volume is not automatically a known predecision plan, and feed composition/density and additions must be established. Do not feed recorded future measurements into a forecast tool. Existing records cannot reveal outcomes of unperformed operating actions or actual transfers. No mAb target is present in the current CHO benchmark.

## Defer while model updates are excluded

Defer different vessel size/geometry, impeller/sparger changes, spatial mixing or oxygen gradients in SMPL, new cell lines/media biology, downstream purification and final product quality, commercial cost/energy, and full-batch PenSim claims. These require missing physics, new characterization, a separately admitted model or independent experimental qualification. A volume-only edit or relabeling a random seed as a new site does not supply that evidence. Relevant upstream limitations include empirical approximations, limited variability/observability and exploitation outside reliable ranges, expressly discussed by the [SMPL authors, Sections 2 and 3.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.08851).

## Evaluation and claim boundaries

Freeze the experiment family, case-selection rule, control envelope, objectives and missingness/numerical gates before inference. Record all selected cases and failures. Hold out simulator realization seeds and operational schedules independently of earlier inspected cases; do not select them by expected B performance. Model calibration and evaluator seeds must remain distinct. Keep source and receiving clocks explicit, and withhold receiving futures, action outcome tables and scores until immutable submission.

**Same-model tool/evaluator scoring is mechanistic simulation evidence only.** Different seeds, nominal parameters or numerical solvers do not make shared equations an independent scientific examiner. It can demonstrate that an agent uses supplied process dynamics to improve a bounded simulated decision; it cannot prove the effect in a real plant. Preserve the empirical CHO forecast as a separate result rather than pooling it into a single simulated/real accuracy score.

Use paired A/B differences with intervals clustered by the underlying process case/EXP; additional AI repetitions are nested within that case. Report prediction intervals and their empirical coverage only where measured targets support that check. Keep uncertainty for **real observational forecasts**, **simulated operating consequences**, and **simulated recipe adaptation** separate. Neither statistical intervals nor good numerical agreement establish actual transfer readiness, avoided batches, manufacturing safety or engineer time saved.
