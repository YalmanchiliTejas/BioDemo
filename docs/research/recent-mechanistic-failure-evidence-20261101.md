# Recent-publication screen: measured mechanistic manufacturing failures

Research date: 2026-11-01. Read `Main_Culture_andETHOS.md`. This is a source screen, not a claim of validated predictive performance. **No source below establishes both a dated 2023–2024 incident and a disclosed million-dollar loss.** Publication dates must not be represented as incident dates. Model suggestions are our interpretations, not models validated in these papers.

## Important near misses

- **Zadravec digital twin:** [SSRN preprint](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5015657) posted **2024-11-09**; [final article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejpb.2025.114662) published **2025-02-10**. [Institutional repository](https://dk.um.si/IzpisGradiva.php?id=92065&lang=eng) mixes a 2024 year field with explicit 2025 receipt/acceptance/publication dates. It couples 3-D equipment CFD and 1-D vials and explores disturbances/failure conditions. This supports simulation capability, **not a documented failed commercial batch**.
- **Wang et al. 2024**, [industrial-perspective penicillin DO feast/famine study](https://doi.org/10.1002/bit.28782): real scale-down experiment with 150-second DO oscillations and reduced productivity, but experimentally imposed conditions rather than a documented named-company production failure. Useful mechanistic data, different evidence category.
- **Najarian et al. 2024**, [Merck vaccine primary-drying optimization](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijpharm.2024.124168): actual experiments and models, intentionally increasing drying aggressiveness to find collapse limits; no established failed production-scale incident in accessible evidence.
- [Jubilant HollisterStier oncology scale-up summary](https://www.pharmaceuticalonline.com/doc/lyo-cycle-development-ensures-success-for-oncology-drug-scale-up-0001): actual excess-moisture validation problem claimed, but visible summary supplies no incident/publication date or numerical moisture/cycle data; full study gated. Do not assign it 2023–2024.

## Practical implication

For a defensible benchmark, separate **historical real failure**, **recent publication**, **controlled mechanism experiment**, and **synthetic model scenario**. The Pfizer vial and Amgen filtration cases are the strongest measured physical failures found in this screen, with explicit recency caveats. Obtaining experimental tables or traces may permit a reviewable reconstruction; it does not turn reconstructed inputs into original plant data.
