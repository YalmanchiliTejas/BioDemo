# External simulator experiment audit

Research check: 27 October 2026. Scope: scientific and numerical semantics for a bounded executable pilot using PenSimPy/IndPenSim and SMPL mAb models. No simulator was installed, modified or executed for this note. These are implementation observations and recommendations, not model qualification or experimental results.

## Experiment interpretation

Use three separate endpoints: (1) forecasts of withheld simulator trajectories, (2) decisions checked by a simulator after commitment, and (3) generalization to explicitly changed simulated receiving conditions. Report them separately. An agent with the exact evaluator equations and parameters has a privileged-model advantage; evaluating its simulation predictions against the same model tests model use and numerical agreement, not independent physical validity. A different numerical solver is a verification check, not an independent scientific model.

Freeze cases and limits before inference. Use matched initial conditions/disturbances across compared actions, record all attempted cases including numerical failures, and keep future states, simulator internals, fault/seed labels, candidate outcome tables and scores outside agent tools. Parameter estimation from partial observations must not silently use hidden parameters. Distinguish known equipment specifications from unknown realized batch parameters. These are recommendations consistent with the [upstream SMPL limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.08851#S3.SS6).

## SMPL: units, boundaries and objective

Inspect the original [environment](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl/blob/main/smpl/envs/mabenv.py), [helpers and physical differential equations](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl/blob/main/smpl/envs/helpers/mab_helpers.py), [documentation](https://smpl-env.github.io/smpl-document/mabenv.html), and [paper Appendix A.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.08851). Original repository URLs currently redirect to `Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl`; freeze an actual commit.

`UpModelHelper.xdot` defines a 17-state upstream bioreactor/separator model. Its physical state order is:

| Index | Quantity | Physical unit |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1, 8–9 | Viable/total cells in bioreactor and separator | cells/L |
| 2–5, 10–13 | Glucose, glutamine, lactate, ammonia | mM |
| 6, 14 | mAb concentration | mg/L |
| 7, 15 | Bioreactor/separator volume | L |
| 16 | Culture temperature | °C |

Seven physical inputs are `F_in, F_1, F_r, F_2` (L/min), inlet glucose/glutamine (mM), and coolant temperature (°C). The ODE time unit is minutes. Recycle assumptions include 92% cell retention and 20% solute/mAb retention. The model does not represent dissolved oxygen or mixing gradients. See [helper definitions](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl/blob/main/smpl/envs/helpers/mab_helpers.py).

The full `MAbEnvGym` adds buffer/capture dynamics: 1,970 states and nine inputs. It divides physical states/inputs by `xscale/uscale`; even `normalize=False` retains that internal scaling. Enabling normalization adds another map to [-1,1]. `dt_itgr` and `dt_spl` are minutes. `observation_name/action_name` list only the upstream 17/7 quantities although the full environment is larger. Its stock `productivity` reward mixes unscaled `xx[6]` and still-scaled `current_observation[14]`; it must not be accepted as a physical mass metric. The default reward is steady-state tracking, not measured product yield. Source: [environment implementation](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/smpl/blob/main/smpl/envs/mabenv.py).

For a limited upstream pilot, use the original physical 17-state dynamics and label the extraction explicitly. Preserve positive recycle flow and volumes. Constant-volume command changes require `F_in+F_r=F_1` and `F_1=F_2+F_r`. A transparent harvested-output metric is the time integral of physical separator concentration × its external outlet flow (`x[14]*u[3]`, mg/min). Add remaining inventory separately if that is part of the frozen endpoint; do not count internal recycle as recovered product. Verify `viable <= total`, nonnegative concentrations, valid temperature/volume ranges and numerical convergence. Recommendations derived from the physical equations, not experimentally established acceptance thresholds.

Receiving-condition tests may vary supplied inlet composition or bounded flow/coolant capability while holding the cell model fixed. These are simulated operating-condition changes. Merely changing reactor volume does not supply missing hydrodynamic or mass-transfer scale-up physics. The helper's thermal term uses a hardcoded 150-kDa mass described as an “mAb cell”; this is an upstream physical assumption needing expert scrutiny, not a validated mammalian-cell heat balance. The [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2206.08851) explicitly acknowledges limited noise/variability, optimistic observation access, incomplete constraints and exploitation outside reliable ranges.

## PenSimPy: units, numerical pitfalls and transfer scope

Primary implementation: [step/reset/controller code](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/peni_env_setup.py), [channel definitions](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/data/batch_data.py), [observation conversion](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/utils.py), [constants](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/constants.py), and [Python ODE](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/ode/indpensim_ode_py.py).

Nominal duration is 230 h, 1,150 steps of 0.2 h. Seven commanded quantities are sugar/oil/gas/water/PAA/discharge flows (L/h) and head pressure (bar). Measured oxygen is mg/L; temperature is K; penicillin/biomass concentrations are g/L; volume is L. Internal `pH` state is hydrogen-ion concentration and becomes pH through `-log10`; final postprocessing changes its representation. Channel labels have other inconsistencies, so derive additional units from equations rather than names alone. Source: [channels](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/data/batch_data.py), [observation conversion](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/utils.py).

The step implementation floors nonpositive values in its first 31 states to 0.001, sets DO below 2 to 1, and replaces nonfinite product/volume values before its yield calculation. Post-step finite/positive checks therefore cannot establish raw solver validity. Check/instrument raw integration results and solver diagnostics; retain all intervention-dependent clipping. Both solver paths extend to nominal endpoint plus `h_ode` (0.005 h); record returned timestamps and check elapsed-time interpretation. Returned `yield_per_run` is not terminal concentration and subtracts discharged product in its incremental expression. Freeze an explicit metric such as terminal penicillin g/L or independently integrated product mass; avoid interchangeable use of yield, mass and concentration. Source: [step implementation](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/peni_env_setup.py).

Changing `random_seed_ref` changes growth/production parameters, initial conditions, transfer coefficient, feed composition and disturbances together. Different seeds represent different batches/conditions, not isolated changes of receiving equipment. The ODE fixes vessel geometry and other process parameters; a volume-only edit is not a demonstrated scale-up model. For a bounded capability experiment, hold seed/material/cell conditions fixed and explicitly vary a declared operating command or characterized parameter. If hidden physical parameters differ between source and receiver, their availability/estimation must be disclosed. Sources: [reset](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/peni_env_setup.py), [ODE](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/ode/indpensim_ode_py.py).

The original [IndPenSim paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0168165614009377), DOI 10.1016/j.jbiotec.2014.10.029, reports historical comparison with ten real 100,000-L penicillin batches under recorded operating inputs. That evidence does not certify numerical equivalence of this Python/C++ port or validity of arbitrary receiving-site interventions. Our [previous methodology](../case_studies/benchmark_methodology.md) recorded failed full-episode numerical/time gates and withheld optimization scores. A new successful gate is required before scoring new episodes; historical failures must remain documented.

## Minimum interpretation of a successful pilot

A complete, reproducible trajectory with passed numerical checks permits reporting performance within that frozen simulator/operating range. Bounded simulated receiving-condition experiments can show whether the agent adapts to declared differences. Neither establishes actual manufacturing transfer readiness, final product quality, regulator acceptance, causal repair or real batches prevented. Validate those uses against independent measurements and qualified scientific review.

## Follow-up: native/Python PenSim RHS comparison

Read-only inspection of local upstream checkouts `/private/tmp/biodemo-fastodeint-source` and `/private/tmp/biodemo-pensimpy-source` found the following. This is source review, not a claim of completed numerical parity tests. Original links: [C++ RHS](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/fastodeint/blob/master/src/main.cpp), [Python RHS](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/ode/indpensim_ode_py.py), [recipe lookup](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/examples/recipe.py), [default recipe](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/data/constants.py).

- The C++ viscosity derivative contains `pow(a_0, (1 / 3))`. Integer division makes this exponent zero; Python 3 evaluates `1 / 3` as a fraction. A specifically documented floating-point-exponent repair can restore agreement with the Python implementation. Preserve the original source/hash and show numerical RHS comparisons across varied positive states and parameter/flag combinations before calling it a verified port repair. Agreement with the Python implementation does not establish agreement with original MATLAB or physical measurements.
- Both versions use parameter index **30** for the viscosity flag. There are five biological parameters followed by 26 control/disturbance/flag values, for **31 total** parameters. This index is not itself a discrepancy.
- Both versions overwrite the `inhib_flag` 0/1-specific inhibition assignments with unconditional full-inhibition expressions. This is a shared source behavior; changing it is a model change rather than a C++/Python parity correction.
- The Python RHS changes `y[6]` in place when pH is at least 7; C++ computes a local transformed hydrogen-ion variable. Evaluate Python reference derivatives on a fresh state copy, test both acidic and alkaline cases, and avoid allowing this mutation to corrupt a SciPy solver's owned state. Other than the cube-root expression and this side effect, this manual inspection found no further algebraic discrepancy. This is not an exhaustive proof.
- The native extension stores its 31 parameters globally, is not safe for concurrent independent episodes in threads, and lacks robust array-length validation. Use processes or serialized native calls behind a shape-checked host interface. Both paths can evaluate fractional powers outside valid physical domains; reject invalid states instead of silently assigning scientific scores.

### Recipe and discharge interpretation

`Recipe.get_value_at` forward-fills the preceding setpoint. Consequently the default profile's `0 at 100 h`, `4000 at 102 h`, `0 at 130 h` means sustained discharge from 102 to 130 h, not a two-hour harvest pulse. A replacement two-hour pulse is a new declared pilot recipe unless independently verified against an upstream original; it should not be described as an established author-intended correction. The controller negates a positive discharge command before sending it to the ODE. The original channel calls it L/h, but the ODE divides it by density in the volume balance and adds it directly in the weight balance, consistent with a signed mass-flow quantity. Retain this metadata/equation conflict explicitly and avoid deriving product-mass claims from the label alone. Sources: [recipe](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/examples/recipe.py), [controls](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/peni_env_setup.py), [channels](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/data/batch_data.py), [ODE](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/ode/indpensim_ode_py.py).

## Concrete bounded pilot v3: read-only scientific audit

Inspected [upstream adapter](../../backend/benchmarks/external_simulators/upstream.py), [case generation/scoring](../../backend/benchmarks/external_simulators/experiment.py), [restricted runner](../../backend/benchmarks/external_simulators/run_agents.py), SDK tool definitions and frozen [pilot-v3 artifacts](../../backend/results/external-simulators/pilot-20261027-v3/protocol.json). This section audits implementation/artifacts, not empirical process accuracy or the agent-performance result.

### Correctly scoped checks

- PenSim endpoints are concentration and dissolved oxygen at **96 h**, with decision cutoff **80 h** for prediction/outcome tasks. This is a deliberately shortened development horizon after the failed 230-h attempt, which must remain disclosed. Harvest commands first occur at 100 h and therefore do not execute during this pilot. No complete-batch optimization or actual transfer result follows.
- SMPL uses the original physical 17-state helper for **24 h**, with a 6-h decision cutoff. Its integrated objective correctly multiplies separator mAb concentration by external outlet flow and integrates over **minutes**, yielding mg. Outcome objectives cover only the 18 h after commitment; transfer objectives cover 24 h. Compare arms within tasks, not raw objectives across those two horizons. Concentration forecasts refer to reactor mAb; output-mass objective uses separator mAb, explicitly different quantities.
- Aeration factors 0.95/0.90 and four balanced-flow factors define declared receiving operating conditions. They do not change vessel geometry or qualify site transfer. SMPL scales all four flows together, retaining the original volume balances. There is no downstream quality/recovery, oxygen/mixing scale-up or commercial economics evaluation.
- Independent checks of all **24 public packets** found no receiving observation later than its decision cutoff, and no receiving observations in transfer packets. Completed source records include later source-clock times; that is legitimate historical context under the separately stated clocks. Source endpoint calibration is calculated from completed source trajectories, not receiving futures. The source and receiver deliberately reuse a realization seed: this creates optimistic reproducibility of hidden biology/material variation and must not be generalized to independently varying plants.
- The recorded qualification gate passes: exact repeatability, tighter-tolerance and alternate-solver agreement, and nonzero response to one command change. Recorded maximum relative terminal discrepancies are approximately 1.45e-8 for PenSim and 4.46e-8 for SMPL. PenSim qualification records 480 integration steps, one positivity-floor event and no DO-floor events. These receipts establish numerical consistency for their tested case, not independent experimental validation of every case or intervention.

### Information-equivalence limitation to resolve or disclose

Public-packet identity and identical shared tool schemas are necessary but do not establish that both arms receive every assumption of B's nominal model:

1. **SMPL's public nominal initial state is not the tool's actual nominal initial state.** The packet publishes unperturbed `xss` under `nominal_source_initial`. `scenario(seed=0)` instead applies random ±5% initial-state multipliers and sets temperature to 36.8°C. Those seed-zero perturbations are used in the frozen nominal trajectory library, but their actual values/generation rule are absent from the packet. A cannot reproduce B's specific nominal trajectory using only the published initial state and equations.
2. **PenSim's nominal realization is incompletely specified for A.** Initial state/parameters and controller code are supplied, but future seed-zero disturbance histories and their complete generation/default configuration are not published in the packet. B accesses the particular trajectory generated using these assumptions. The code must not be described as a pure computation-only difference without this limitation.
3. B's tool returns **precomputed nominal action metrics** from a frozen library, rather than dynamically executing an arbitrary model request. It supplies four permitted action calculations one at a time, with no hidden actual-seed outcome or score. This is still model-derived assistance, but should be described accurately. Its product and indicator forecasts receive source-only corrections; its reported objective remains uncalibrated, as the implementation comment states.

Report the current run as an exploratory comparison of packet analysis with access to prepared nominal forward-model results. For a strict same-information comparison, freeze a **new** dataset exposing the actual nominal initial state, complete nominal configuration/disturbance assumptions and command construction to both arms. Do not change packets or tool behavior during a frozen run or choose changes based on receiving targets/performance. Source-calibrated model advantage remains limited evidence because tool and evaluator share equations and the candidate set is tiny.

### Denominators and remaining interpretation limits

There are two realization seeds × two capabilities × three tasks × two simulators = 24 paired packets, or 48 agent attempts. Tasks reuse the same realization/capability and KEEP trajectories, so these are not 24 independent process experiments. No power calculation or untouched holdout is established. Scoring measures forecast errors and objective improvement/regret on a finite action grid; it does not establish manufacturing safety or feasibility across unspecified quality constraints. Preserve failures and separate non-submission from wrong forecasts. The correct-clock and exponent changes remain disclosed port/driver corrections; the source-model physical assumptions remain unqualified.

## Final frozen pilot: audit qualification after the v3 fixes

Read-only recheck of `backend/results/external-simulators/pilot-20261027-final` (protocol version `external-simulator-pilot-4-shared-nominal-assumptions`), `forward.py`, the updated host tool, and public packets confirms that the material nominal-assumption mismatches identified above were addressed **before this new comparison**, rather than patched into the earlier frozen dataset:

- SMPL nominal runs no longer use random initial perturbations. The public nominal state is the exact unperturbed upstream state with temperature **36.8°C**, matching the tool's nominal initialization. Actual receiving/source cultures retain their separately generated synthetic variation.
- PenSim nominal disturbances are disabled with `Dis=0`, explicitly published to both arms. Public packets contain 33 initial state values, 31 initial parameter values, control flags, recipe, controller/step source, and stated clock/exponent corrections. Actual receiving disturbances remain unknown rather than silently being supplied to B.
- The B tool now launches a **fresh nominal simulator subprocess for each requested action** through [forward.py](../../backend/benchmarks/external_simulators/forward.py). The worker receives a public packet and the external source/build locations, derives simulator/capability/task/action, runs `nominal=True`, then applies the source-only calibration. It receives no actual realization seed or evaluator target. Worker environment construction omits inference credentials. The host checks each live prediction against the frozen nominal reproducibility record before returning it. Thus the earlier cached-lookup description applies to the superseded pilot, not this final tool.
- Rechecking all **24 final public packets** found zero receiving cutoff violations and no receiving observations in transfer packets. Recorded isolation probes deny evaluator, source-runner, model-library and network access from general numerical Python, with no credential variables present. These probes and the SDK allowlist support the stated boundary; they are not a whole-host security proof.
- The final numerical qualification receipt passes repeatability, tighter tolerances and alternate solvers for the recorded qualification cases. No new simulator execution or performance-based tuning was done during this audit.

No additional material hidden-realization/answer-key exposure or units/objective blocker was identified in this final read-only check. A remaining reproducibility limitation is that the provided PenSim controller source calls `pid_controller` without including that helper's definition in the packet. Parameters and intended physical assumptions are shared, but the packet is not a complete independently runnable upstream distribution. Do not claim exact self-contained implementation parity merely from matching packet hashes. The helper's implementation remains available in the pinned external [utils.py](https://github.com/Mohan-Zhang-u/PenSimPy/blob/master/pensimpy/utils.py); access to a working simulator is the intended computational advantage of B.

Final interpretation remains bounded: a small, inspected, synthetic, shared-equation experiment with correlated tasks; PenSim ends at **96 h**, SMPL at **24 h** upstream. Both arms receive the same historical evidence and declared nominal assumptions, while only B can invoke the provided live nominal tool. Report actual completed/failed attempts, each forecast target and action outcome separately. Numerical success does not establish experimental accuracy, full-batch optimization, product quality, scale-up, site-transfer readiness, safety or real engineering savings. Earlier failed/superseded artifacts and their audit notices remain relevant development history.

## Corrected treatment: AI with versus without a supplied physics model

The preceding both-arms-equations comparison tests executable access, rather than the user's intended comparison against AI without a supplied model. It must remain a separate historical/secondary experiment. The corrected main A/B treatment is implemented by [treatments.py](../../backend/benchmarks/external_simulators/treatments.py) and the revised restricted runner. This section is a **pre-inference code/projection audit**, not a new result claim.

Independent read-only checks projected all **24 frozen cases** through `common_evidence` and `assert_no_supplied_model`. Every projected packet preserved source/receiving observations exactly; no receiving-time cutoff violation appeared, and transfer packets remained empty of receiving measurements. The whitelist retains recorded source observations, operating commands/schedules, known receiving capabilities, units, available actions and evaluation objectives. Actual operating settings are legitimate common evidence even where their original source was a simulator configuration.

Both arms now receive the same frozen projected packet bytes. The projection removes physical/kinetic equations, model coefficients/initial-state specifications, calibration, solver corrections, controller/step source and nominal predictions from **both prompts**. A retains general numerical Python, with no loaded nominal-result library or supplied physics packet. B alone can call the live nominal physics tool, which internally retains the physical configuration and source-derived calibration. This is a deliberate model-assisted treatment; calibration derived from source evidence is part of that treatment, not hidden receiving ground truth.

The host writes B's full model input to `host-model-input`, a sibling outside the ordinary numerical scratch directory. The numerical sandbox permits only its own scratch plus interpreter/dependency roots; it denies workspace/temporary-source files and explicitly denies installed `fastodeint`, `pensimpy` and `smpl` paths. The new isolation procedure tests installed native-simulator import. Additional import probes for both Python simulator packages and a direct sibling host-model-file read are useful preflight verification of those policies; this audit did not execute live inference or modify code/data. SDK builtin filesystem, shell, memory and child tools remain disabled, with one immutable submission and no evaluator feedback before commitment.

No treatment-purity blocker was identified in this code/projection review. The correct label is **AI without a supplied physics model**, not AI without any physics knowledge: pretraining and independently derived numerical models cannot be excluded and are allowed baseline reasoning. Compare A versus B as the headline, using identical models, budgets, source evidence and receiving inputs. A separate model-only reference, if calculated, is secondary. All earlier scientific limitations remain: 96-h partial PenSim, 24-h upstream SMPL, shared evaluator equations, small correlated development cases, ideal observations, and simulated operating-condition transfer rather than validated plant/site transfer.
