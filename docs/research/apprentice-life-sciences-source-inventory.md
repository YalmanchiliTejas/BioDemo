# Apprentice life sciences public offering: source inventory

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-23. **Scope:** Apprentice official public pages linked from its Life Sciences page and official product/platform pages. This is a clean-room capability inventory, not a claim about private implementation.

## How to read this inventory

“Documented” means the official page describes the capability. It does not mean independently tested, generally available, or validated for a particular customer. “Claim” marks quantified outcomes, superiority, compliance, speed, or quality language that the source asserts but this research did not verify. No proprietary internals are inferred.

## Capability inventory

### Life sciences portfolio (LS)

- **LS01 — Documented:** life sciences framing spans product development and manufacturing; page names biologics, cell therapies, and mRNA.
- **LS02 — Documented:** electronic signatures, audit trails, role-based access, and validation pack are presented as platform capabilities.
- **LS03 — Claim:** adaptable workflows and templates support different therapy types, batch sizes, and process changes.
- **LS04 — Documented:** APIs/no-code integrations are described for ERP, QMS, historians, equipment, sites, and partners.
- **LS05 — Documented:** life-science agent roles listed include Operator, Quality, and MSAT (among broader team/site roles).

### Manufacturing Execution System (MES)

- **MES01 — Documented:** no-code recipe authoring by SMEs; drag-and-drop branching, parallel steps, and enforcement rules.
- **MES02 — Documented:** parameterized templates; mid-run recipe/parameter changes, batch redlining, and not-applicable steps.
- **MES03 — Documented:** web, mobile, and hands-free execution; automated capture, guardrails, calculations, and exception notifications.
- **MES04 — Documented:** site rollout/configuration and validation workflow; page describes multi-site activation and QMS integration.
- **MES05 — Claim:** “90 days” to go live and six sites in nine months; deployment speed is not independently verified.

### Laboratory Execution System (LES)

- **LES01 — Documented:** no-code test methods, assays, and protocols with calculations, limits, and guided media.
- **LES02 — Documented:** sample tracking from collection to result, generated IDs, plate assignment, and chain-of-custody visibility.
- **LES03 — Documented:** instruments, materials, and lab activities connected; multi-user access and role-specific permissions.
- **LES04 — Documented:** centrally versioned test methods with local adaptation; execution can be embedded with manufacturing.

### Connected Manufacturing Network (CMN)

- **CMN01 — Documented:** cross-site recipes, resources, batches, enterprise/site views, and granular access controls.
- **CMN02 — Documented:** centrally managed, parameterized templates distributed across internal and external sites.
- **CMN03 — Documented:** transfer package includes batch templates, master data, resource mapping, and audit trail.
- **CMN04 — Documented:** sponsor/partner access can be view-only or interactive; batch review and approval workflows.
- **CMN05 — Documented:** lifecycle overview groups products by stage/market and tracks readiness, exceptions, and review state.
- **CMN06 — Claim:** one-click transfer and months-to-hours transfer reduction are promotional assertions, not measured here.

### A1 agent and agentic AI (A1)

- **A1-01 — Documented:** configurable agents/workflows define repeatable steps/actions; agents can produce editable manufacturing artifacts (e.g. reports, CAPAs, deviations).
- **A1-02 — Documented:** event, schedule, alarm, and file-change triggers; described channels include email, SMS, WhatsApp, Slack, and Teams.
- **A1-03 — Documented:** system connectivity is described across ERP, MES, QMS, EAM, SCADA, historians, PLCs, IoT, and flat files; Webhooks, MQTT, and OPC-UA are named.
- **A1-04 — Documented:** named agent roles include Operator, Quality, MSAT, Supervisor, Engineering, Maintenance, Supply Chain, Manufacturing Systems, and Quality Systems.
- **A1-05 — Documented:** security page says AI features are admin-controlled and humans remain final decision-makers for GMP-related actions.
- **A1-06 — Claim:** “24/7,” “fault-tolerant,” “always available,” and model-performance comparisons are vendor assertions; no independent measurement was reviewed.
- **A1-07 — Unknown:** model internals, grounding/retrieval design, evaluation protocol, failure behavior, approval granularity, and customer-specific availability are not established by public pages reviewed.

### Ganymede / R&D and manufacturing intelligence (GAN)

- **GAN01 — Documented:** product page describes a data cloud for capturing, standardizing, and automating lab/process data.
- **GAN02 — Documented:** sources named include instruments, ELN/LIMS, apps, OPC interfaces, PC files, and historians.
- **GAN03 — Documented:** modular analyses, no-code AI workflow modules, visualization, and investigation tools are described; examples include curve fitting, qPCR, and chromatography.
- **GAN04 — Documented:** listed use areas include bioprocess/DOE, chromatography, liquid handlers, plate assays, and equipment/OEE analytics.
- **GAN05 — Unknown:** deployment topology, supported connectors by version, transformation semantics, lineage detail, and integration boundaries with Tempo/A1 are not specified in the product overview.

### Digital work instructions (DWI)

- **DWI01 — Documented:** authoring supports branching, validation rules, reusable templates, and collaboration/version rollout.
- **DWI02 — Documented:** PDFs/paper procedures can be converted with AI authoring; operators get step guidance, prompts, images/video, and web/tablet/wearable execution.
- **DWI03 — Documented:** step timestamps, optional electronic signatures, approvals, and quality-agent exception monitoring are described.
- **DWI04 — Claim:** conversion “in minutes,” improved accuracy, and reduced errors are stated outcomes, not independently verified.

### Virtual remote collaboration (VRC)

- **VRC01 — Documented:** remote video collaboration via web, mobile/iPad, or wearable headset.
- **VRC02 — Documented:** live drawing, AR arrows/laser pointers, transcription, walkie-talkie, and chat tools.
- **VRC03 — Documented:** external SMEs/vendors can view a first-person equipment perspective; page describes permission settings, enterprise logs, and SSO.
- **VRC04 — Claim:** reduced delays, travel, and costs are stated benefits without measurements in the reviewed page.

### Integrations (INT)

- **INT01 — Documented:** no-code configuration wizard/templates connect applications and equipment; automatic shop-floor capture is described.
- **INT02 — Documented:** public Web API and event-driven subscription; examples include inventory creation, consumption, and transfer.
- **INT03 — Documented:** ERP bidirectional exchange covers master data, production runs, lots/expiry, material consumption, and activity visibility.
- **INT04 — Unknown:** public overview does not enumerate a complete connector matrix, API schema/version lifecycle, throughput limits, or per-connector data ownership/error semantics.

### Security, compliance, and services (SEC / SVC)

- **SEC01 — Documented claim:** official page states SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001:2022 certification; it also cites cGXP/21 CFR Part 11, GDPR, SSO, MFA, permissions, monitoring, and human oversight. Certification evidence/scope was not independently checked.
- **SEC02 — Documented:** product pages describe electronic signatures, audit trails, access controls, validation materials, and controlled AI permissions.
- **SEC03 — Unknown:** public pages reviewed do not establish tenant isolation, encryption/key management details, backup/restore objectives, data residency, model data retention, or a certification report’s scope.
- **SVC01 — Documented:** delivery outline covers align/scope, configure processes/materials/equipment and train users, validate, then launch/hypercare.
- **SVC02 — Documented:** support pages cite user guides, tickets, live support; services page cites validation documentation and scheduled releases.
- **SVC03 — Claim:** “manufacture in 90 days or less” and low annual upgrade burden are vendor assertions; actual scope and conditions are not specified.
