## Model/credit recommendation

The observed model was `zai-org/GLM-5.3` on the existing Modal route. The gateway
reports zero **estimated token cost** because its self-hosted compute is unpriced;
actual provider billing remains unknown. No claim of zero cost or a reconciled
campaign total is made. Further model retries were stopped after the repeated
empty-answer failures. No new paid compute or OCR-weight downloads were started.

First evaluate an explicitly configured lower reasoning effort for routine typed
workflow planning. The current provider request has no explicit native reasoning
selection, and two live calls consumed their output allowance without answer content.
This is a hypothesis to test against the existing endpoint, not a proven fix or a
setting changed in production. The same phenomenon occurred in earlier GLM research
calibration; see the retained physics-value runner documentation.

A smaller planner may also be suitable for schema-bound workflow assembly and a
smaller interpreter for routine explanations. Keep deterministic solvers, evidence
checks, approval boundaries and the complex-investigation route. Compare candidates
on the same frozen cases: valid plans, correct tool choice, completed workflows,
unsupported claims, clarification behavior, latency and actual billed cost. Lower
reasoning on the same model and a smaller model are separate conditions. Do not
claim quality is preserved until measured. No production model was changed.
