# Governing product direction

Read [Main_Culture_andETHOS.md](Main_Culture_andETHOS.md) before making design, product, or architecture decisions. The user has designated this new document as the governing source, replacing the earlier Main_Culture_ideology.md and its interpretation. Do not substitute generic design preferences, skill defaults, or earlier design notes for its direction.

Apply all four pillars jointly: Easy to Start, Easy to Trust, Easy to Act, and Easy to Adopt. These are product-wide principles, not a prescribed workflow, persona, interface, competitor strategy, or roadmap. Do not constrain the product to fermentation investigations or assume an agent-first, chat-first, or dashboard-first solution.

Before meaningful implementation or redesign, follow the document's engineering decision rules: define the job and context, assess all four pillars, compare simple and ambitious alternatives where trade-offs are significant, preserve interoperability and evidence, choose observable measures of real-world effort, and state trade-offs. Inspect existing code and constraints before recommending replacements.

Skills may guide execution only when consistent with this document. Earlier design documents describe historical implementations; they do not override it. Choose interactions and architecture according to user intent and task complexity. Preserve permissions, provenance, explicit human authorization for consequential operations, and existing systems of record. Do not claim validated results or compliance without evidence.

Use judgment within these principles; they do not prescribe every implementation detail. Clarify consequential unresolved requirements with the user while continuing independent authorized work.
